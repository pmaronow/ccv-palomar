This inventory maps the paper's 32 primary numbered statement blocks to the substantive Lean development. It records reviewed statements and their scope, not a claim that every literal clause of all 32 blocks has been independently certified. The comparison is an automated mathematical statement review. [VERIFICATION.md](VERIFICATION.md) records mechanical checks for the prepared source revision; compilation and axiom checks alone do not certify a translation from prose to Lean. The [machine-readable inventory](coverage.json) gives qualified declaration names and source modules.

The submission selects five targets: Theorem 1.1, the exponent limit and polynomial bounds of Corollary 1.2, Theorem 1.3 and Theorem 1.4. Their concrete definitions and intentional theorem placeholders are in [Challenge.lean](../Challenge.lean); [Solution.lean](../Solution.lean) supplies corresponding proofs using the development.

The table uses these scope labels:

- **Selected exact target:** the submission statement was compared to the manuscript and the named proved endpoint. The noted domain-quantifier limitation remains part of the statement.
- **Quantitative target:** the displayed mathematical estimate or identity was checked against the named declaration's hypotheses and conclusion. Supporting construction details can reside in other modules.
- **Conditional target:** a proved general theorem under explicit primitive assumptions; those assumptions must be discharged for a statistical application.
- **Components:** supporting proved declarations were inspected, but the entire literal numbered statement was not independently reassembled as one exact target in this review. This label does not assert a counterexample or a hole in the selected headline proofs.

## Targets, assumptions and background

[PaperGoals](../NearlyMinimax/PaperGoals.lean) defines proposition-valued targets such as `MainBracketClaim`, `UpperSharpClaim`, `LowerSharpClaim`, `LowSmoothnessClaim` and `ParametricClaim`. Defining a target is not proving it. The public statistical modules named above supply proofs in their stated model and dimension/smoothness regimes. Analytic consequences with an `_of_mainBracketClaim` suffix remain conditional infrastructure until specialized to `paper_main`.

[HighCompleteCostRisk](../NearlyMinimax/HighCompleteCostRisk.lean) and [HighCompleteCostFlexible](../NearlyMinimax/HighCompleteCostFlexible.lean) accept a spatial envelope and Fisher/series budget as sufficient premises. The actual saddle witness supplies them. [PaperScoreRisk](../NearlyMinimax/PaperScoreRisk.lean) instead names the actual compact-nuisance norm and discharges those bounds internally. Neither route treats a desired minimax conclusion as a model assumption.

The local nuisance norm takes the supremum over legal density values, coefficient ball, external mean and variance **before** spatial integration. Conditional centering is based on response-law normalization and genuine density-action cancellation. Torus representatives `[0,1)` and closed patch cubes `[-1,1]^d` differ from the paper's charts on null boundaries; exact chart modules prove the h^d measure factor. The lower construction uses Borel density versions and standard Borel marks, as specified in the paper.

The sharp bracket still leaves `(log n)^Gamma`. The exact logarithmic power, optimized leading constants, useful explicit thresholds and moderate-sample performance are not established. Claims concern constant conditional variance with the stated strict density/variance/fourth-moment margins and Hölder extension class; they do not cover heteroskedastic estimation. Abstract localization has the countably generated sigma-algebra qualification, and the selected low/parametric statements have the fixed-domain qualification above.

The manuscript's literature comparisons and cited books/articles remain mathematical background. Proving the finite moment rules, an alternative hyperplane field, marked-history machinery and selected statistical endpoints does not formalize every bibliographic or unnumbered claim. Declaration counts and trusted-axiom inventories are not paper-coverage counts.
